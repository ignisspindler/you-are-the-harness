# The Epistemic Airlock
*Run it before any important AI claim enters your project. Two to three minutes.*

```
  CLAIM ──► ┌─────────────────────────────────┐ ──► IN or OUT
             │ 1. What EXACTLY is the claim?   │
             │    (state it precisely)         │
             │ 2. Did I believe this BEFORE    │
             │    the AI said it?              │
             │ 3. What would make it FALSE?    │
             │    (if you can't answer: pause) │
             │ 4. What's an ALTERNATIVE        │
             │    explanation?                 │
             │ 5. Does anything OUTSIDE this   │
             │    conversation support it?     │
             └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

Not because the AI is untrustworthy. Because fluent language collapses observation, inference and interpretation into a single smooth surface, and the five questions separate them again.

## The quadrant it guards against

```
                    EXTERNAL SUPPORT
               LOW                HIGH
          ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
 HIGH     │  BEAUTIFUL      │    STRONG       │
 FIT      │  ECHO           │   SYNTHESIS     │
 TO YOU   │    (danger)     │    (goal)       │
          ├─────────────────┼─────────────────┤
 LOW      │  IRRELEVANT     │   SURPRISING    │
 FIT      │  NOISE          │   CORRECTION    │
 TO YOU   │  (easy discard) │  (valuable)     │
          └─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

The dangerous quadrant is the Beautiful Echo: it fits your worldview perfectly and may have no external support at all. The better your harness works, the better the AI gets at producing these. Question 5 is the structural defense.

## The autopsy, for a sentence that sounds wise

Take it apart, one layer at a time: What is the observable fact? What is the inference, the leap from fact to conclusion? What is the interpretation, the framing that makes the inference seem inevitable? What is the rhetoric, the word choice that makes you want to believe it? What evidence is missing that would confirm or refute it?

## When the claim is a fact

Questions 3 and 5 have a concrete form: find it somewhere the AI did not write. Open the source. If the AI gave you a citation, check that it exists and says what it is claimed to say. AI tools produce references that look entirely real and are not. Trust the document you opened, never the reference to it.
