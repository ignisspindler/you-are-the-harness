# The six roles
*How to generate broadly without surrendering the synthesis.*

Do not ask "what is the answer?" Ask "attack this from six directions." Then you do the synthesis, not the AI.

```
           Your Claim
                │
    ┌───────────┼───────────┐
    ▼           ▼           ▼
 EXPAND     COMPRESS    ATTACK
    │           │           │
    ├───────────┼───────────┤
    ▼           ▼           ▼
 HISTORY    TRANSLATE   ARCHITECT
    │           │           │
    └───────────┴───────────┘
                │
          YOU SYNTHESIZE
```

## The roles

Six epistemic functions, not six personas with names.

```
EXPANDER     "What possibilities are we overlooking?"
             → options you didn't consider

COMPRESSOR   "What is the simplest version of this idea?"
             → the core, stripped of noise

ADVERSARY    "What is specifically wrong with this?"
             → structural weaknesses, real objections

HISTORIAN    "What does this resemble? What has been tried before?"
             → precedent, pattern, cautionary examples

TRANSLATOR   "How would [a specific audience] understand this?"
             → reframing for comprehension or persuasion

ARCHITECT    "How should the pieces be ordered?"
             → structure, sequencing, flow
```

The Adversary is the most skipped and the most valuable. A well-run Adversary tells you what your critics will say before they say it to your customer, your manager, or your reader.

## The synthesis table, always yours

```
  ┌────────────┬────────────┬─────────────────┐
  │  RETAIN    │   REJECT   │   INVESTIGATE   │
  │ useful,    │ misaligned,│ uncertain,      │
  │ confirmed  │ wrong, or  │ needs real-     │
  │            │ not mine   │ world evidence  │
  └────────────┴────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

Never automatically combine the outputs. Let contradictions stay visible. Something must go in Reject; if everything looks good, you are not reading critically enough. Anything in Investigate leaves the session and meets the real world before it enters your project.

## Taste is often "no"

A healthy workflow throws away most of what is generated. That is not failure. That is selection: raw material in, finished work out, waste expected.
