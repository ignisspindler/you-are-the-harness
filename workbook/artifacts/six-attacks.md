# Six-Attack Synthesis
*Built in Module 03. One claim, six angles, and a synthesis table that is always yours to fill.*

**The claim:**

## The six attacks

**EXPANDER** (what possibilities are we overlooking?)

**COMPRESSOR** (what is the simplest version of this idea?)

**ADVERSARY** (what is specifically wrong with this?)

**HISTORIAN** (what does this resemble? what has been tried before?)

**TRANSLATOR** (how would a specific audience understand this?)

**ARCHITECT** (how should the pieces be ordered?)
