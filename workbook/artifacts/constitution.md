# Project Constitution
*Built in Module 01. Your tutor fills this in from your own words. Paste it at the start of any session on this project.*

**1. WHAT am I actually trying to accomplish?**
*(The outcome, not the deliverable. What will be true in the world when this is done?)*

**2. WHY does it matter? What outcome equals success?**

**3. NON-NEGOTIABLES: what must stay true even if everything else changes?**

**4. OPEN QUESTIONS: what am I genuinely uncertain about?**

**5. WHAT I DO NOT WANT: attractive drift directions**
*(The most important field. Directions that would look like progress from the outside but would actually be drift.)*

**6. AUDIENCE: who is this for, and what do they need to feel or understand?**

**7. TASTE: what should it feel like, and what should it absolutely not feel like?**

**8. CURRENT THESIS: what do I presently believe about the best approach?**

**9. PERMISSION TO CHANGE: what evidence or experience would justify changing my mind?**
