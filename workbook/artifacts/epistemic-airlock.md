# Epistemic Airlock
*Built in Module 04. Run it before any important AI claim enters your project. Two to three minutes.*

Personalized for my role and project:

1. **What EXACTLY is the claim?** (state it precisely)
2. **Did I believe this BEFORE the AI said it?**
3. **What would make it FALSE?** (if you cannot answer, pause)
4. **What is an ALTERNATIVE explanation?**
5. **Does anything OUTSIDE this conversation support it?**
